2025 to 2026

Multi-agent task allocation

My master’s research at Georgia Tech’s Advanced Manufacturing Pilot Facility: splitting one toolpath between several robot arms without them colliding.

The G-code is cut into contours with a layer dependency graph. Each free arm claims the best available contour; a cached ellipsoid prefilter with a reach check, and a learned pairwise model, skip most of the expensive collision checks. If an arm drops out mid-job, the schedule replans around it.

On the bench: two Tormach ZA6 arms on pedestals, drawing on a whiteboard level with their bases. The cell here runs the same idea live.

Python, ROS 2, PyTorch, python-fcl, MuJoCo

pyrobopath fork and PairwiseCollisionNet
